The premise

You got out of prison owing more.

Nobody will hire you. A loan shark offers you a loan at “reasonable interest”. The only door still open is a casino’s.

Nobody says “reasonable” when it is.

You get ₡500. You pay back ₡88,888,000, in 15 installments that climb like a staircase: ₡1,000, ₡2,000, ₡5,000… up to ₡50,000,000. Each installment gives you three days.

On the last day of an installment, you pay it in full or that’s as far as you go.

The 15 installments go from ₡1,000 to ₡50,000,000.

How to play

A day at the casino

Every day is one turn of the clock: 150 seconds to move around, three bets to risk your money and, at the end, the collector.

DAY 1Time

2:30

Bets
Cash₡500
Debt₡1,000

The clock runs even if you don’t play.

  1. 2:30 · THE CASINO OPENS

    A hundred and fifty seconds

    Walking doesn’t cost bets, but the clock keeps running. Eating, going to the restroom or seeing the Brujo eat into it too. Learn the casino and you get to play more.

  2. THREE BETS

    Four tables, three bets

    Only playing costs a bet, and you get three a day. The ones you don’t use are lost. Every game has its own room, and it pays a little more there.

  3. BETWEEN TABLES

    The Brujo and his pins

    The Brujo, the casino’s fortune-teller, sees what today’s luck has already decided. He says it halfway, and sometimes he’s wrong. He also sells pins: they don’t work miracles, they cheat a little.

    You can wear up to five on your shirt. Each pin’s card tells you what it does; how they combine, you learn by losing.

    Hover over a pin, or tap it, to see its name.

    • Unusual

    • Forbidden
  4. A DRINK FOR THE NERVES

    The bar

    With a few drinks in you, luck seems on your side, the floor moves and your body reacts late. The bartender knows when it’s enough. You never do.

    In the smoking room, a cigarette speeds you up. It costs time, not bets. Your lungs get billed by someone else.

  5. 0:00 · END OF THE DAY

    The collector arrives

    When the day closes, he comes for his cut. You can pay whenever you want; on the last day of an installment, you pay it in full or that’s as far as you go.

Survived? Tomorrow you owe more. And if you do too well, the organization moves you to another casino, with a new floor plan to learn.

The casino

Learn it: it won’t change until they move you.

Each casino is put together piece by piece from a seed and stays the same until you’re moved. Knowing where every room is buys you time against the clock. This is the one from seed 777, straight from the game.
