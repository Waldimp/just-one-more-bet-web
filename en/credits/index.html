Original art

The logo —the poker chip reading “just one more bet” and its spinning animation— is the game’s own art, by Walter Daniel Mejía Palacios and Samuel Fernando Calderón Reyes. It’s the one this website and its favicon use.

The casino façade and its lion, the Las Vegas sign, the parking lot, the pins, the blackjack table, the food, the smoke, the signs and many of the props are the team’s own hand-drawn art. A few backgrounds —the bar, the food counter, the intro scenes and the horses— were generated with PixelLab and retouched by hand.

The night version of the parking lot that opens this website was built from the game’s art, with its same palette.

Music

The intro theme is the team’s own music: slow casino jazz, with no third-party samples.
